Modules

Eight modules, one quiet interface.

Everything a library help desk does — from finding a free desk to releasing the ones nobody claimed.

01

Sign in with your 8-digit registration number. Students and admins each land on their own dashboard.

Sixty seats across three zones, drawn row by row, with live state for any date and slot.

How it works

From empty chair to auto-release in four steps.

  1. Log in

    Register once with your registration number, then log in from any device in the library.

  2. Pick a slot & seat

    Choose a date and a two-hour slot, then tap any outlined seat on the map to book it.

  3. Check in on arrival

    Check in from 10 min before the slot until 15 min after it starts.

  4. Seats free themselves

    No-shows are released automatically and finished sessions end on time, so seats are always shared fairly.
